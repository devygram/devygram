### Overview
**Kashio** is a modern personal finance and subscription manager designed for clarity, control, and privacy. It tracks daily expenses, visualizes monthly recurring commitments, alerts you before renewal dates, and provides instant executive AI insights.

### Key Highlights
- **Interactive Subscription Calendar:** View renewal dates and monthly commitments on an interactive visual grid.
- **AI Financial Summaries:** Executive 2-sentence financial health digests powered by Gemini 1.5 Flash.
- **Cash Flow Analytics:** Weekly spending velocity charts, category allocation breakdowns, and payment method diagnostics.
- **Obsidian Dark Aesthetic:** Sleek, high-contrast dark mode tailored for focus and productivity.
